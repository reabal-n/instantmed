# Google Ads Optimizes Net Retained Purchase Value

Status: accepted; adjustment mechanism amended 2026-08-16

InstantMed uses the server-side offline purchase import as the Primary Google Ads bidding signal, not browser purchase tags or funnel micro-conversions, because Stripe/Supabase payment truth is the least ambiguous conversion source. The chosen optimization target is **Net Retained Purchase Value**: successful purchases upload once by intake/order id, while exact refund cash and durably terminal-lost dispute cash produce aggregate `RESTATEMENT`s of the retained AUD value. Internal revenue preserves exact zero; Google receives a reversible A$0.01 floor at zero so a later refund reversal or dispute reinstatement can restore value. A `RETRACTION` is deliberately not used because it makes the conversion irreversible. Every adjustment generation records a PHI-safe `google_ads_conversion_adjustment` audit row and is owned by a durable claim/lease.

Considered alternatives: optimize against gross purchase value and handle refunds only in internal reporting; assign dollar values to page view / intake complete / checkout start milestones; or retract a fully lost order. The first two make Google Ads chase low-quality or reversed revenue. Retraction was retired because later refund reversals and dispute reinstatements must be able to restore the order's value. Micro-conversions stay Secondary/non-bidding and PostHog remains the product-funnel source of truth.
